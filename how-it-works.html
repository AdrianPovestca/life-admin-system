How Punctlife works

Punctlife is designed to take the mental load off. It doesn't try to do everything; it focuses on keeping your everyday administrative life in one calm place.

Step 1 — Open your workspace

Visit the application and create your account. There is no complicated setup, no app store required, and no credit card needed upfront. Your personal dashboard is ready instantly in your browser.

Step 2 — Add what matters

Start recording the things that usually live in your head. Add a bill that needs paying, an upcoming medical appointment, a document you need to find, or a letter from an institution you need to reply to.

Step 3 — Stay on top of things

Use your dashboard to see exactly what needs your attention today. When something is done, you check it off. The system is built to be reviewed for a few minutes each day, so you can stop worrying about what you might be forgetting.

Access it anywhere

Because Punctlife is a web application, it works wherever you have a browser. You can open it on your phone while waiting in line, or on your laptop at your desk. Learn how to add it to your devices.
